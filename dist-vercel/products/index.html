Packaging buying guide

Match the box to the food

Hot, oily food needs a vented, grease-resistant box. Soft or cold food like cakes needs a rigid, flat-bottomed box.

Size to the portion

A box that is too large lets food slide around; too small crushes it. Use the size guide below, and ask us to suggest a size for your menu.

Vented vs sealed

Vents let steam escape so fried food stays crisp. Sealed boxes keep food warm longer — pick based on what you serve.

Plain vs printed

Plain boxes are cheapest and ready to ship. Printed boxes carry your brand to every customer and pay back over larger runs.

Order for the season

Festival weeks and weekends spike demand for cake and broast boxes. Order a few days earlier so stock is not an issue.

Store them dry

Keep boxes off the floor and away from damp walls. Humidity softens the board and makes boxes harder to fold.

Which cake box size should I choose?

A ½ kg cake usually fits an 8×8×5 in box, a 1 kg cake a 10×10×5 in box, and a 2 kg cake a 12×12×6 in box. Choose a taller box for tiered or decorated cakes.
